Case Study

Arafat Sarkar

Building the first trusted digital portfolio for a modern cinematographer with Django and Nuxt.

Django Vue.js PostgreSQL
Problem

The Challenge

Arafat Sarkar needed a first official online presence that matched the quality of his cinematography work. Without a dedicated platform, potential clients had no central place to view projects, understand creative style, or submit booking inquiries.

Approach

The Strategy

We created a cinematic portfolio platform centered around visual storytelling. The experience was designed to showcase reels, commercial work, and project highlights while making it easy for agencies, brands, and production teams to connect.

Engineering

What Was Hard

The challenge was presenting large media assets in a fast and elegant way. We built an optimized Django backend for content management and a Nuxt frontend with lazy loading, image optimization, and smooth page transitions.

Delivery

What We Built

  • First official portfolio website
  • Dynamic reel and project showcase pages
  • Inquiry & booking request system
  • SEO-friendly public pages
  • Optimized media delivery for fast loading
  • Automated backups and deployment workflow
Experience

Product & UX

A decoupled system using Django REST Framework and Nuxt 3, hosted on AWS. The interface used cinematic dark tones, generous spacing, fullscreen imagery, and minimal copy to keep attention on the visual work.
