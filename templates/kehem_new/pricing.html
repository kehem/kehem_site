Pricing Questions

Questions, answered

Are these fixed prices?

They are starting points. Final pricing depends on scope, content, integrations, product complexity, and timeline.

Can we start smaller?

Yes. We often begin with a focused audit, landing page, prototype, or MVP slice before a larger investment.

Do you work globally?

Yes. KEHEM works remotely with clients across countries using structured calls, async updates, and clear delivery milestones.

What makes KEHEM premium?

We combine polished interface taste with serious engineering: stable systems, clean architecture, and conversion-aware presentation.

How long does a build take?

Most websites take 2-4 weeks. SaaS and custom software usually take 6-12 weeks depending on features, integrations, and review speed.

How do payments work?

Most projects start with a deposit, then milestone payments. Monthly teams are billed on a recurring retainer.

Do we need content ready?

Not always. We can work from rough notes, old pages, or a product brief, then help shape clearer copy and structure.

What happens after launch?

We can stay on for fixes, improvements, monitoring, SEO updates, feature sprints, and long-term product support.
